Privacy

How wrytr treats
your data.

We collect only the information needed to keep the product reliable and help you write in your own voice.

01

What may be stored

Email, account data, voice settings, writing samples, and materials you choose to add to the product or submit through the early-access form.

02

How it is used

This data supports app access, draft generation, your personal voice profile, and product-related communication.

03

What we do not do

We do not sell your writing, publish your materials without permission, or turn personal samples into public templates for other users.

04

Control and deletion

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your data. Just write to us from the address connected to your account.
